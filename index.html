1. Hydraulic Resistor Network

L(D) · p = b   |   Qij = (Dij / Leff) · (pi − pj)

Each tube acts as a fluid pipe whose conductance is D / Leff. A Jacobi-preconditioned Conjugate Gradient solver finds node pressures satisfying Kirchhoff flow conservation at every junction.

2. Multi-Terminal Tokyo Rail (2010)

I0 = 0.65 √(K / 2)   |   Qrms = √(⟨Q2⟩)

In Tero et al. (Science, 2010), city hubs act as food terminals explored via random pairs while light masks high-cost barriers like Tokyo Bay, balancing cost (TL/MST) and fault tolerance (FT).

3. Vascular & Microfluidic Murray's Law

rp3 = Σ rc3   |   D ∝ r4, μ = 4/3

Under Hagen–Poiseuille scaling (r ∝ D1/4) and non-saturating power feedback at μ = 4/3, vascular trees equalize wall shear stress across branches for tissue scaffolds and heat-sink plates.

4. Soft-Robotics Puncture-Tolerant Actuator

Performance = FT / (TL · MD)   |   D ≥ 10−5

Sub-Murray exponent (μ = 1.15) retains redundant loops across compliant pneumatic channels. Sever any primary supply line with Cut Edge to observe pressure-driven bypass regrowth.

5. Shortest Path & Lyapunov Mirror Descent

E(D) = ½ (Σ ce De + bT p)   |   dD/dt = |Q| − D

Bonifaci et al. (2012) proved linear Physarum dynamics monotonically minimizes Lyapunov energy E(D) via mirror descent, converging onto the exact weighted shortest path without explicit Dijkstra search.

6. Capacitated Min-Cost Flow & Crossbar

ceff = ce (1 + λe)   |   λ̇ ∝ (Qpred − ue)

Following Straszak & Vishnoi (2016), dual congestion prices λe reroute overflow around capped corridors onto longer paths while pruning unused memristor crosspoints to OFF for SPICE export.

Upper: λe(D, Q)  →  Lower: No-Overlap + Setup

Upper-layer Physarum balances a 7-stage jewelry workflow (Wax → Casting → Filing → Setting → Polish → Plating → QC) and feeds shadow prices λe to the Lower-Layer FJSP decoder.
